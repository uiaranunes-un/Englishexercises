Question word + am / is / are + subject?
What is = What’s · Where is = Where’s · Who is = Who’s · When is = When’s

Personal information: name · age · address · phone number

1. Choose the question word

Complete with What, Where, Who, How or When.

2. Complete with am, is or are

Complete the questions.

3. Put the words in order

Write the complete question. Example: you / are / where → Where are you?

4. Write the question

Use the words in brackets. Example: (where / she) → Where is she? — She’s at home.

5. Complete the conversation

Write the missing words. Use the profile below.

Mia Carter
Age: 26 · Now: at home
Address: 42 King Street
Phone number: 202-555-0176

6. Your turn

Write a short conversation. Meet a new classmate. Ask their name, age and how they are. Write the answers, too.
