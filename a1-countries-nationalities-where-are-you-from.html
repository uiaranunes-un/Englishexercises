Remember

Use a country after from:

He’s from Brazil.
Ele é do Brasil.

Use a nationality after be:

He’s Brazilian.
Ele é brasileiro.

He, she, it

he → ele
she → ela
it → usado para coisas, lugares ou animais

He’s from Mexico.
She’s from Japan.
It’s from Portugal.

Asking about country

Where is he from?
He’s from Brazil.

Where is she from?
She’s from Canada.

Yes / No questions

Is he from Brazil?
Yes, he is. / No, he isn’t.

Is she from Japan?
Yes, she is. / No, she isn’t.
