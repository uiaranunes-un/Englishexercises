B1 — Families and the Future

Family vocabulary and future forms: will, be going to, present continuous and shall.

Family Vocabulary — Quick Reference

only child — a person with no brothers or sisters
single-parent family — a family in which one parent raises the child or children
stepmother / stepfather — a parent's new wife / husband
half-brother / half-sister — a sibling who shares one parent with you
in-laws — your husband’s or wife’s family
extended family — grandparents, aunts, uncles, cousins, etc.
generation — people in a family who are at a similar stage of descent
relatives — people who belong to your family
great-grandparents — your grandparents’ parents
couple — two people who are married or in a romantic relationship

3. Grammar Reference — Talking about the future

English has several ways of talking about the future. The choice depends on how the speaker sees the future event.

Form We usually use it for... Example
will + base verb predictions based on opinions or expectations I think families will become smaller.
will + base verb decisions made at the moment of speaking I’ll call Grandma now.
will + base verb promises and offers I’ll help you with the children.
be going to + base verb intentions or decisions already made We’re going to move closer to my parents.
be going to + base verb predictions based on present evidence Look at those boxes! They’re going to fall.
present continuous future arrangements that are already organized We’re having dinner with my cousins on Friday.
Shall I...? / Shall we...? offers and suggestions with I or we Shall we invite your parents too?
Compare:

We’re going to visit my grandparents this weekend.
→ We have decided to do it. The important idea is the intention.

We’re visiting my grandparents at 11 on Saturday.
→ The visit is already arranged.

I think we’ll visit them more often in the future.
→ This is a prediction or expectation.

Remember: Sometimes more than one future form is grammatically possible, but the meaning or emphasis changes.

Ask yourself: Is this an opinion, an intention, an arrangement, a decision made now, or a prediction based on evidence?
